The D3+K2 Synergy: Bones & Eyes

Vitamin D3 increases calcium absorption from your gut into your bloodstream. But without K2 (specifically MK-7), that calcium can deposit in arteries instead of bones. For eyes, D3 receptors are present in the retina, supporting photoreceptor function, while K2 protects the delicate micro-vessels supplying the optic nerve.

💡 The Foundation Rule: D3+K2 is non-negotiable for anyone over 30. It's the base layer of both bone density and long-term vision preservation.

Why "D3 Alone" Can Be Dangerous

Many people supplement with high doses of Vitamin D3 (5000 IU+) without K2. While this boosts blood levels of D3, it creates a "calcium paradox": calcium is absorbed rapidly but has nowhere to go. Over time, this can lead to arterial calcification (hardening of arteries) rather than stronger bones. K2 acts as the traffic cop, directing calcium exactly where it belongs.

The Retinal Connection

Recent studies show that Vitamin D deficiency is linked to dry eye syndrome and age-related macular degeneration. The retina has a high concentration of D3 receptors. When combined with K2's vascular protection, this duo supports the intricate blood supply needed for sharp vision.
